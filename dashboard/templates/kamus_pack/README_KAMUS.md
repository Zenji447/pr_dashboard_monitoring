# Pack Kamus

## Nombre recomendado
Kamus

## Concepto
Kamus es la mascota original de una app web de despliegues. Su personalidad es rápida, confiable, divertida y pro.

## Prompt maestro
Create an original mascot character named “Kamus” for a software deployment web application. Kamus is a cute cloud-tech mascot with a rounded fluffy blue cloud-shaped head, a soft light face area, large expressive shiny eyes, dark eyebrows, and a cheerful smiling mouth. He has a playful, friendly, energetic personality and should feel fast, reliable, fun, and smart.

Kamus wears white gloves and rounded white boots with blue accents. On his chest, include a cloud-shaped badge containing a minimal deployment/server icon made of three horizontal bars and small status indicator dots, with one subtle green status light. The design should look clean, modern, polished, vector-like, web-friendly, and visually appealing for SaaS branding.

Style: cute mascot, polished vector-cartoon, smooth shading, soft highlights, crisp clean edges, modern UI-branding style, centered composition, full body, isolated character, transparent or white background.

Important: keep the character original and distinctive, suitable as the official mascot of a deployment platform. Do not make it identical to any existing mascot or brand character.

## Negative prompt
blurry, low quality, extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed hands, duplicate body parts, complex background, text, watermark, photorealistic style, dark horror style, messy composition, inconsistent costume, off-model character, cropped feet, cropped hands

## Prompt serie completa
Create an original mascot character named “Kamus” for a software deployment website. Kamus is a cute cloud-tech mascot with a fluffy rounded blue cloud-shaped head, a soft white face panel, big expressive shiny eyes, dark eyebrows, a cheerful smile, white gloves, and rounded white boots with blue accents. He has a cloud-shaped badge on his chest with a minimal deployment/server icon made of three horizontal bars and small status lights, including one green indicator. Kamus should feel fast, reliable, playful, fun, and trustworthy.

Generate a consistent series of illustrations for a website animation or GIF. Keep the character design, proportions, colors, face, gloves, boots, and chest badge consistent across all frames. Use a clean vector-cartoon style with polished shading, modern SaaS-branding aesthetics, centered composition, and a transparent or white background.

Create these scenes:
1. Kamus running energetically as if rushing to execute a deployment.
2. Kamus dancing in a playful celebratory pose.
3. Kamus wearing a cowboy outfit, dancing in a funny and charming way.
4. Kamus wearing a ninja outfit in a dynamic stealthy action pose.
5. Kamus celebrating a successful deployment with a joyful victory pose and a subtle green success symbol.
6. Kamus in a deploy-in-progress pose, focused and active, interacting with a subtle progress indicator.

Keep the illustrations suitable for a modern website and make the character fully original.

## Prompts por frame

### 1. Kamus corriendo
Kamus running energetically as if rushing to complete a deployment, cheerful expression, dynamic forward motion, one leg forward and one leg back, arms pumping, full body, original cloud-tech mascot, clean vector-cartoon style, white or transparent background.

### 2. Kamus bailando
Kamus dancing in a playful and funny way, cheerful expression, one leg raised, lively celebratory pose, full body, original cloud-tech mascot, clean vector-cartoon style, white or transparent background.

### 3. Kamus vaquero
Kamus dressed as a cowboy, wearing a cowboy hat, red bandana, and cowboy boots, dancing or tipping the hat in a fun playful pose. Keep the same original Kamus identity, same face, same body proportions, and the same cloud-shaped deployment badge on his chest. Clean vector-cartoon style, full body, white or transparent background.

### 4. Kamus ninja
Kamus dressed as a ninja, wearing a dark ninja outfit and headband, in a playful agile action pose as if moving quickly through a deployment mission. Keep the same original Kamus identity, same face, same body proportions, and the same cloud-shaped deployment badge on his chest. Clean vector-cartoon style, full body, white or transparent background.

### 5. Kamus deploy successful
Kamus celebrating a successful deployment, joyful victory pose, both arms raised or showing a thumbs up, happy smile, with a subtle green success check icon near him. Keep the same original Kamus identity and chest badge. Clean vector-cartoon style, full body, white or transparent background.

### 6. Kamus deploy in progress
Kamus in a deploy-in-progress pose, focused and active, interacting with a subtle floating progress indicator or loading icon, friendly but concentrated expression. Keep the same original Kamus identity and chest badge. Clean vector-cartoon style, full body, white or transparent background.
